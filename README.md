# The Old World Simulator

A dice-level simulator for **Warhammer: The Old World** combat.

## Intent

1. **Named characters** — build combatants from published profiles (Korhil, Ishaya Vess, …) with their real rules.
2. **Custom characters** — build your own within the constraints of the rulebook: legal profiles, legal equipment options, legal special-rule combinations.
3. **Duels** — resolve a fight between two characters by rolling actual dice, following the Old World combat sequence, and report the outcome.

Units, shooting, movement and psychology are out of scope for now — see [todo.txt](todo.txt).

## How a duel resolves

`combat_simulation(c1, c2, rounds)` → per round:

0. In the first round only, **Impact Hits** land before any blow (at the mount's Strength for a mounted model). The engine does not track who charged, so (as with Furious Charge and lances) both fighters count as charging in round 1.
1. `determine_strike_order` splits the fighters into **strike steps**. Strike First always precedes normal, which always precedes Strike Last; Initiative only breaks ties *within* a band. Both bands and Initiative equal → one step, i.e. simultaneous. Strike First and Strike Last on the same model cancel out. Weapon-granted Strike Last (a Great Weapon) counts as well as the character's own rules.
2. Every strike in a step is **rolled before any of it is applied**, so simultaneous fighters both get to swing.
   `OneRoundMeleeCombat` = `apply_weapon_stats` → `RollToHit` → `RollToWound` → `RollArmorSave`, returning a `StrikeResult` and applying no damage.
3. `resolve_strike` is the **single place** wards, regeneration and Killing Blow are resolved. Each unsaved wound gets a ward save, then a regeneration save; what survives either slays outright (Killing Blow) or costs one wound.
4. Deaths are checked after each step, so a fighter killed before it swings does not swing. A mount strikes at its own Initiative, in its own step, and always attacks the other fighter.
5. **Stomp Attacks** come last, after every other attack, each round. Impact Hits and Stomp Attacks hit automatically at the model's unmodified Strength (`AutomaticHits`); a behemoth's stomps have AP -2 except against another monster (Thunderstomp).

Within a step, all damage is applied before any Wound Stealing heals, so it does not matter which fighter is passed first.

`Character.Wounds` is the profile maximum and is never mutated. All damage goes to `Character.current_wounds`.

### Rules implemented

Ithilmar Weapons, Ensorcelled Weapons, Gromril Weapons, Warpstone Weapons, Accursed Weapons, Monster Slayer (a Killing Blow against monsters), Cleaving Blow (a natural 6 denies armour and Regeneration saves to infantry, cavalry and war beasts), weapons that deny Regeneration (`No Regeneration Saves`) or carry their own Strength (`Strength (4)`), unit wards (Runes of Protection, Runes of Warding, Dark Runes, Relentless Warriors, Daughters of Eternity, Blessings of Ulric, Blessings of the Horned Rat), Inner Circle, wards against magical attacks only (`Ward5 (magical)`), Khopesh, Obsidian Blades, Gromril Armour, Flammable (no Regeneration save against a Flaming attack), Multiple Wounds with a dice value (`Multiple Wounds (D3)`, rolled per unsaved wound), the unarmoured 7+ armour value (a shield or Armoured Hide gives an unarmoured model a 6+), the named wards of the later lists (Arcane Shield, Blessed Knight, Favour of the Goddess, Celestial Forged Armour, Talismanic Tattoos, Ancestral Shield, Blessings of the Volcano God vs Flaming), Settra's Champion (Nekaph's Killing Blow on a 5+, since a duel is a challenge), Magical Attacks, Reroll Hits 1, Hatred(X) (plural-tolerant, so `Hatred (Dwarfs)` matches `Race: "Dwarf"`), Frenzy, Furious Charge, Choppas, Elven Reflexes, Ethereal, Killing Blow (character- or weapon-granted, with a parsed threshold), Armour Bane (`AB1` or `Armour Bane (1)`), Flaming, Magic, ward saves (WardX, Chaos Armour (X+), Witness to Destiny, Dragon Armour, Blessings of Asuryan vs Flaming — each recognised bare or with a `(6+ Ward)` gloss), Regeneration (`Regen5` or `Regeneration (5+)`), shields, armour bonuses (`AH1` or `Armoured Hide (1)`), Wound Stealing (the Bog-wood Staff), forced rerolls of a successful enemy hit (Mathlann's Ire), Strike First / Strike Last, and the first-round gating on lances, cavalry spears, flails and morning stars.

Added with magic items and abilities: Poisoned Attacks (a natural 6 To Hit gives +2 To Wound, not with a magic weapon), Impact Hits and Stomp Attacks (fixed or dice amounts), Thunderstomp, dice-valued Extra Attacks (rolled once per round), To Hit modifiers (`To Hit (+1)`, `Enemy To Hit (-1)`; a natural 1 always misses and a natural 6 always hits), re-rolling failed hits or wounds, re-rolling wound rolls of 1, enemies re-rolling successful hits, wounds or armour saves, `Wounds On (2+)`, `Cannot Be Wounded On 2`, `No Armour Saves`, `Immune to Multiple Wounds`, armour that cannot be improved or modified, `Improve Armour (N)`, `Improve Armour Piercing (N)`, Strength-limited wards (`Ward5 (Strength 5+)`), the maximum armour value (2+ for infantry and cavalry, 3+ for chariots, monsters and war machines), Marks of Khorne (Frenzy), Nurgle (enemies re-roll 6s To Hit), Slaanesh (+1 Initiative in the first round) and Tzeentch (Flaming Attacks), the Grail Vow (Magical Attacks), the Blessings of the Lady (6+ Ward, 5+ against Strength 5 or more — assuming the army prayed), Beguile / Allure of Slaanesh, Virtue of Audacity, and characteristic modifiers from items and abilities.

### Rules present in the data but inert

Army-, psychology- and movement-level rules that a one-on-one duel never exercises: Gaze of the Gods (a Command sub-phase roll, and there is no command phase), the Mark of Chaos Undivided, Warp-spawned, Unstable, Unbreakable, Handler, Loner, Vanguard, Accomplished Archers, Arrows of Isha, Commanding Voice, Naval Discipline, Valour of Ages, Waaagh!, Warband, Da Boyz, Mob Rule, Ignore Panic, Ignore Goblin Panic, Fear of Elves, Rallying Cry, Impetuous, Quell Impetuosity, Animosity, Immune to Psychology, Stubborn, Unbreakable, Stupidity, Terror, Large Target, Close Order, Valour of Ages, Move Through Cover, Evasive, Ignores Cover, Ambushers, Fast Cavalry, Swiftstride, Hit & Run, All Sneaky Like, Chariot Runners, Warpaint.

Rules that a duel *would* exercise but that are not implemented yet are listed by `python3 run_tests.py --gaps`, which reads them from `tests/rule_catalogue.py` - the catalogue the test suite holds the data to.

All of them are carried verbatim on the profiles, so nothing is lost when they are implemented.

Also inert: mounts are recorded on profiles but not yet used. `OptionalRules` and Marks of Chaos are applied when chosen (see the desktop app, or pass them as `SpecialRules`).

## Naming

Factions can be named by alias. `resolve_faction` accepts the army book's own
title as well as the internal key, matching case-insensitively and treating
`&` as `and`, so all of these reach the same roster:

```python
Character(name="Grimgor", faction_type="Orc & Goblin Tribes", profile_name="Black Orc Warboss")
Character(name="Grimgor", faction_type="O&G",                 profile_name="black orc warboss")
Character(name="Grimgor", faction_type="Orcs",                profile_name="Black Orc Warboss")
```

The internal key stays `"Orcs"`, which is what `FactionProfiles` is keyed on and
what a built character reports as `.faction`; the resolved profile name is on
`.profile_name`. Profile names are matched case-insensitively too, since the
published names mix `Orc BigBoss` with `Black Orc Bigboss`. Profiles take shorthand names too, via `PROFILE_ALIASES`: `"Korhil"` reaches
`"Korhil Lionmane"`, `"Handmaiden"` reaches `"Handmaiden of the Everqueen"`,
`"Kiknik"` reaches `"Kiknik Toofsnatcha"`. Add new aliases to `FACTION_ALIASES`
or `PROFILE_ALIASES` in [faction_profiles.py](faction_profiles.py) — tests check
that every alias points at something that exists.

## Adding a faction

Each faction is one module in [factions/](factions/), declaring:

| name | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `FACTION` | the key it is filed under in `FactionProfiles` |
| `ALIASES` | other names that should resolve to it |
| `PROFILE_ALIASES` | shorthand names for individual profiles |
| `CHARACTERS` | character profiles |
| `UNITS` | regular unit profiles (`UnitCategory: "Unit"`) |
| `PROFILES` | `CHARACTERS + UNITS`, which is what the registry reads |

Drop a module in the package, list it in `FACTION_MODULES` in
[factions/__init__.py](factions/__init__.py), and nothing else needs to change:
`faction_profiles.py` assembles the profiles and both alias tables from it.
`TestFactionPackage` checks every module declares the required names and that no
two factions claim the same alias.

`RACE_NAMES` stays central, because races cut across factions — a Goblin appears
in an Orc army, and Hatred (Orcs & Goblins) has to match both.

## Adding a profile

Profiles come from the army pages at `https://tow.whfb.app/army/<slug>` and the per-unit pages at `https://tow.whfb.app/unit/<slug>`, which carry points, wargear options and special rules.

The ten factions added last (Grand Cathay, Kingdom of Bretonnia, Lizardmen, Ogre Kingdoms, Realms of Men, Regiments of Renown, Skaven, Tomb Kings of Khemri, Vampire Counts, Wood Elf Realms) were drafted with `python3 tools/transcribe_faction.py <key> -w` and reviewed by hand. Corrections the site's own row cannot express (Settra's Wounds come from his chariot; the Anvil of Doom fights with its crew) are `overrides` in the generator's config, with the reason written into the profile comment, so re-running it is safe. Weapons with no melee profile (bows, pistols, breath and thrown weapons) are left out of the options and named in the profile comment.

Regular units come from the same pages and the same generator. A unit's page carries several statline rows - rank and file, champion, mount, crew, beasts - so the generator records:

- `base_profile`: the row that fights - the first rank-and-file row with its own Weapon Skill (a cavalry rider, a chariot's crew, a monster). Where that row prints Toughness and Wounds as '-', they come from the chariot or war machine body. A war machine fights with its crew's T and W.
- `champion`: the row the options let one model be upgraded to; `other_profiles`: every other row.
- `points` (per model unless `points_per` is `"unit"`), `unit_size`, and `points_note` where the site gives the cost as text ("+10 points", "5 points per Skink Handler, 60 points per Razordon").
- The weapon: a unit with a hand weapon and a special weapon fights with the special one (White Lions with their great blades). Weapons marked `Secondary Attack` - tails, maws, Troll Vomit, which make one extra attack a turn - are never the default. If the default weapon is two-handed, the unit's shield is set aside (`Shield: False`) but stays an option.
- The site's printed `armourValue` (chariots, monsters, war engines, Settra) replaces any armour from the equipment: 6+ light, 5+ heavy, 4+ full plate, 3+ `Armour Value 3+`.

Pages that are only a character's mount (dragons, griffons, chariot mounts) are left to `mounts.py`. For the nine hand-written factions, `transcribe_faction.py <key> -w` replaces only `UNITS` and appends any `FACTION_RULES` entries the units add.

Three things to watch when adding one:

- **Rule names must match the constants in [special_rules.py](special_rules.py) exactly.** A typo does not raise — it silently disables the rule. [tests/rule_catalogue.py](tests/rule_catalogue.py) must account for every rule string in the data, so a typo fails the suite.
- **Paid upgrades are not base rules.** Frenzy and Warpaint on an Orc Warboss are bought, so they belong in `OptionalRules` (or get passed via `SpecialRules=` at construction), not in `base_profile["SpecialRules"]`.
- **The site writes statlines as M WS BS S T W I A Ld, but the profile dicts order Initiative before Wounds.** Transposing those two is easy and silent. `TestOrcProfiles.test_statlines_match_the_source` pins every statline against the source.

`TestOrcProfiles` also checks that every profile builds, fights, declares a Race that exists in `RACE_NAMES`, and that its own default weapon and armour satisfy its own `equipment_options`.

## Magic items

Every magic item and ability on the site is in `MagicItemDict`: 751 generated
entries (`magic_items_data.py`) plus the hand-reviewed ones in
`magic_items.py`, which take precedence. "Ability" entries hold Elven Honours,
Knightly Virtues, Wood Elf Kindreds, Chaotic Traits and Gifts, Daemonic Gifts,
Vampiric Powers, Big Names, Runic Tattoos and the like; runes are items in
their own categories.

Each entry has a `status`:

| status | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `reviewed` | transcribed or corrected by hand |
| `applied` | every effect sentence was turned into rules the engine reads |
| `partial` | some were; `not_modelled` lists the rest |
| `not modelled` | it matters in a duel, but nothing was converted |
| `no duel effect` | standards, arcane items, spells, shooting, army rules |

`tools/transcribe_items.py` converts wording conservatively: a sentence that is
conditional (a charge turn, once per turn, a mount, a unit, single use) is never
applied. "During a challenge" counts as always true, because a duel is a
challenge. Hits made *instead of* attacking are not the bearer's attacks, so
the Bloodskull Pendant's Killing Blow is not applied to its normal attacks.

**Buying.** `Character(..., magic_items=[...])` equips any character: a magic
weapon becomes its `Weapon`, magic armour and shields replace its own, and
rules and characteristic modifiers are added (kept between 1 and 10).
`check_purchase` holds a profile-based character to its army's items and the
common ones, to its points per budget (`item_allowances.py`: magic items,
runes, Daemonic Gifts, Gifts of Chaos, Chaos Mutations, Forest Spites,
Vampiric Powers), to pick limits for abilities without a points cost (one
Elven Honour, one Knightly Virtue, two Chaotic Traits...), and to one item per
type, except runes and armour "worn with other armour". A custom fighter may
take anything that exists.

Named characters carry fixed wargear, applied automatically at construction
from `equipment_options["items"]`; their personal items are not for sale.
Some abilities only apply as equipped (Virtue of Heroism needs a mundane
weapon, Enhanced Reflexes a plain hand weapon, Unnatural Fortitude no heavy
armour).

Marks of Chaos and Chivalrous Vows replace one another: passing
`SpecialRules=["Mark of Khorne"]` removes the default Mark of Chaos Undivided,
and only marks the profile offers are accepted.

## Mounts

`Character(..., mount="Griffon (High Elves)")` puts a character on one of the mounts its profile offers. `mounted.py` follows the rules text (Characters & Cavalry Mounts / Chariot Mounts / Ridden Monsters, the Split Profile rules, Challenges & Mounts):

- the model takes the mount's troop type (so Killing Blow, the 3+ armour cap and Thunderstomp follow it);
- cavalry keep the rider's armour, +1 for barding; monsters and chariots use the better of the two armour values;
- "+N" Toughness and Wounds are added; a chariot uses the higher Toughness and adds the character's Wounds to its own;
- the mount (or a chariot's crew and beasts, `(x2)` rows counted) attacks with its own WS, S, I, A and weapon, at its own Initiative, always at the other fighter;
- enemy To Hit rolls use the rider's WS; Impact Hits and Stomp Attacks use the mount's Strength;
- special rules are shared both ways, except rules marked "(but not its mount)", hand-weapon rules, High Elf Strike First, the rider's magic items, and rules a profile scopes to one mount ("Armour Bane (1, Chompa only)").

Some mounts need an Elven Honour (`HONOUR_MOUNTS`), and some honours restrict mounts ("may only be mounted on", "cannot be mounted"). Characters whose own page includes their mount - the Dragon Mage, Kiknik, Harald Gemunsen, the Shugengan, Lady Élisse, the Green Knight, Settra - always ride it (`INTEGRAL_MOUNTS`), and their printed Toughness and Wounds are not increased again.

Abilities can unlock equipment: an Elven Honour's sword of Hoeth, ceremonial halberd or Chracian great blade, Blood of Caledor's full plate, a Knightly Order's wolf hammers or lances. The item's `grants` field is read from its text, and a character may take the weapon once it has the ability. Bows are recorded but not offered, since shooting is not simulated.

## Scraping caveat

**Use `tools/tow_site.py`.** Every page on `tow.whfb.app` embeds the full
entry it renders as JSON (`<script id="__NEXT_DATA__">`), so the helper reads
that with `curl` instead of the rendered page, and caches it under
`.tow_cache/`. Either the entry is there or the helper raises — including for
the site's soft failure, where an unknown slug under a valid route returns
HTTP 200 with an empty entry. The notes below predate the helper and apply to
reading the *rendered* page.

The site's own data has errors too, which the tools report rather than copy:
the Saurus Oldblood and Scar-Veteran pages link "Cold One" to the Terradon
page, and the Goblin Warboss and Goblin Bigboss pages link a mount as "Gigantic Spide".

`tow.whfb.app` renders through JavaScript, and a fetch of it can come back
partial — several magic-item pages returned nothing but "Loading...". A partial
fetch is not always obvious: a page can yield its prose paragraph while its
**"Special Rules:" line is missing entirely**, which reads as a complete answer.

That happened here. Chayal was briefly recorded as having no Killing Blow and
no Requires Two Hands, and Mathlann's Ire as having no Armour Bane, because two
independent fetches of those pages both returned only the prose. Both are wrong;
the data now matches what the pages actually show.

So: **treat any rule list taken from a magic-item or special-rule page as
unverified until a human has looked at the page.** The unit/roster pages have
been more reliable — their statlines and special rules matched hand-entered data
everywhere they overlapped — but the item pages have not.

Items whose rules came from a single fetch and have not been eyeballed:
Storm's Wrath, Da Boss's Trophy Rack, Bog-wood Staff, Trollhide Shawl,
Lore Familiar, Pelt of Charandis.

Items whose pages **would not load at all** carry `"verified": False` and an
empty rules list, so they are recorded and testable but contribute nothing:
Armour of Skaldour, Furnace Hammer, Griffon Helm, Grudge-Settler, Grudgestone,
Judgement, Rivet Gun, Slayer Crown. `magic_items.unverified_items()` returns
that list and `TestUnverifiedItems` pins it, so the gap cannot grow unnoticed.
Where a weapon's profile was unavailable the character wields a plain hand
weapon rather than invented stats — Burlok Damminson and his Furnace Hammer.

## Edition

Everything here is **Warhammer: The Old World**, not an earlier edition of
Warhammer Fantasy. All profiles, points, weapon profiles and rules text come
from <https://tow.whfb.app> or from the Arcane Journals it cites.

The engine originally carried assumptions from an earlier edition. Those found
and corrected so far:

| what | was | The Old World |
| --- | --- | --- |
| To Hit chart | two wrong cells (WS4 v WS8, WS10 v WS5) | fixed and pinned |
| To Wound chart | four cells wrongly "cannot wound" (S2/T7, S3/T8, S4/T9, S5/T10) | a gap of 5 still wounds on a 6+ |
| Killing Blow | allowed an Armour save | permits **no** Armour save |
| Killing Blow | allowed a Regeneration save | permits **no** Regeneration save |
| Killing Blow | applied to every model | **Infantry and Cavalry only** |

The charts are the load-bearing ones — every roll goes through them, so six
wrong cells skewed every result. `tests/test_charts.py` now pins all 200 cells.

The maximum armour value is now per troop type, as the rules say: 2+ for infantry and cavalry, 3+ for chariots, monsters and war machines. Poisoned Attacks add +2 To Wound on a natural 6 To Hit (not an automatic wound).

The generic weapon profiles (Great Weapon, Halberd, Flail, Morning Star, Lance,
Cavalry Spear, Whip, Two Hand Weapons) have now been checked against the
site. The Flail was missing Requires Two Hands and now has it. The Flail and
Morning Star also have Armour Bane (1) on the charge only. The engine cannot
limit Armour Bane to the first round, so it is left off both rather than
applied every round.

## Army-wide rules

Each faction module carries a `FACTION_RULES` dict giving its army-wide rules,
each with a `status` of `"implemented"`, `"partial"` or `None` (recorded only)
and the rules text where it could be read. That makes the gap between "the
faction has this rule" and "the engine does something with it" explicit and
testable rather than a matter of reading the engine.

## Known gaps

- **Units fight as a single model.** Ranks, supporting attacks, crew counts and beasts pulling a chariot are not simulated.
- **Mounts: what is not modelled.** Monsters fight with one weapon (secondary attacks such as a tail are not made), Blood Frenzy and Wilful Beast are not simulated, and an Elven Honour that *requires* a mount (Blood of Caledor) does not force one.
- **Random Attacks.** Units whose Attacks are a dice value (Giants, Chaos Spawn, Mangler Squigs...) are recorded with no attacks.
- **Monsters fight with one weapon.** Secondary attacks (tails, maws) are not made.
- **Magic and shooting are not simulated**, so wizards fight as their melee profile and ranged magic weapons (bows, the Rivet Gun) do nothing.
- **Who charged is not tracked.** Charge-round effects (Impact Hits, Furious Charge, lances) apply to both fighters in round 1.
- **Engineers' runes** (weapon and engineering runes) are sold to Engineers only.
- **The Blessings of the Lady** assume the Bretonnian army prayed for them.
- Item effects the generator could not convert are listed per item (`status`, `not_modelled`); `python3 run_tests.py --gaps` lists the special rules that are not simulated.
- Three site entries are deliberately absent: the Wood Elf Beast Pack (a Beast Keeper plus beasts, each its own unit), the Fanatic (it never fights in combat) and the Herdstone (scenery). `tools/verify_rosters.py` records why.

## Desktop app

Double-click `run_app.command` in Finder, or run `./run_app.command`. The app uses native macOS controls and follows light or dark mode.

- **Duel / Units** switches between character duels and unit fights. Rank-and-file combat is not simulated yet, so units fight as a single model; the Units screen says so. Unit size and width are saved for later.
- **Fighter cards** hold army, model, name, weapon, armour, **mount** and shield. Only legal choices are offered; the shield switches off with a two-handed weapon; a character that always rides its mount shows it fixed. The statline, special rules, extras and the mount's attack profile are shown below.
- **Extras…** opens upgrades, Marks of Chaos and Vows, and the **item shop**: tabs per item type (Weapons, Armour, Talismans, Enchanted Items, Arcane Items, Standards, each rune type, each ability group), a **Common items** switch that shows or hides the rulebook items, search, live points budgets and each item's rules text. Items say whether they are partly simulated, not simulated, or have no effect in a duel. Taking an ability that unlocks a weapon (Warden of Saphery's sword of Hoeth) adds it to the card's weapon list.
- **Odds** fights many times and shows a win bar; **Play-by-play** fights once with a colour-coded dice log and switches Rounds to 1 (Odds gets its own value back). **Seed** makes either repeatable; ⌘↩ fights.
- **Save…** keeps a fighter (extras and mount included), listed under **★ Saved** in the army picker. Saved fighters live in `~/Library/Application Support/TheOldWorldSimulator/custom_characters.json`; set `TOW_SIM_CHARACTERS` to use another file.

The launcher uses uv's Python 3.13 (Tk 8.6) and falls back to `python3`.

## Testing

```bash
python3 run_tests.py              # quick tier, ~15s - what the pre-commit hook runs
python3 run_tests.py --full       # large samples + every-profile round robin
python3 run_tests.py -k mirror    # only tests whose name contains "mirror"

```

| `test_rule_catalogue.py` + `rule_catalogue.py` | Every rule string in profiles, weapons, items and honours is classified as *engine*, *label*, *not simulated* or *inert*. Rules filed as having no effect must leave a seeded duel byte-for-byte unchanged, and rules the engine reads must change one. `FACTION_RULES` statuses must agree with the engine. |
| `test_invariants.py` | Every character and unit, in every legal loadout: builds, fights a mirror match, survives all-1s and all-6s dice, fights one opponent per faction (the full tier plays everyone against everyone). After every duel, wounds stay in range, stats and rules are restored, and quiet mode prints nothing. Seeded duels are reproducible. |
| `test_edge_cases.py` | Zero rounds, zero attacks, fighters that cannot wound, double kills, clamped characteristics, the 2+ save cap, unknown kit, self-inflicted misfires. |
| `test_mirror_matches.py` | Identical fighters win equally often, and the argument order of `combat_simulation` does not change the odds. |
| `test_rule_statistics.py` | Each rule's hit, wound, save and ward rates against hand-computed exact probabilities; better characteristics never make a fighter worse. |
| `test_magic_items.py` | Every site item is recorded with a status its effects agree with; every purchasable item is bought and fought with; buying rules; items whose wording is easy to misread (Bloodskull Pendant, Crystal of Kunlan...). |
| `test_honours.py` | Marks of Chaos, Vows, the Blessings of the Lady, and bought abilities, with exact probabilities. |
| `test_mounts.py` | Mounted characters: armour, Toughness and Wounds, shared and rider-only rules, honour limits, integral mounts, strike order, and every character on every mount it may take. |
| `test_monsters.py` | Impact Hits and Stomp Attacks ordering, unmodified Strength, Thunderstomp, dice amounts, Extra Attacks. |
| `test_site_rosters.py` | Every roster, statline and points value against the site (skipped without the `.tow_cache/` page cache). |
| `test_app_model.py` | The desktop app's logic: loadouts, extras, the item shop, runs, saved fighters. `TOW_GUI_TESTS=1` also opens the window. |

Statistical tests use fixed seeds and four-sigma bands, so they are deterministic and do not flake.

**After a major addition**, run `python3 run_tests.py --full` and add tests alongside the change:

- **New faction or units:** add a `RosterCase` with statlines and points. The sweeps in `test_invariants.py` pick the new profiles up automatically.
- **New rule in the data:** implement it, or file it in `tests/rule_catalogue.py`. The suite fails until you do.
- **New engine rule:** add scripted-dice tests for its mechanics, an exact-probability test in `test_rule_statistics.py`, and its constant to `ENGINE_RULES`. Mark it `implemented` in `FACTION_RULES`.
- **New weapon or item:** give it a profile. `test_invariants.py` builds and fights every offered weapon; `test_magic_items.py` every purchasable item.
- **Site data changed:** rerun `tools/transcribe_items.py -w` and `tools/transcribe_allowances.py -w`, and `python3 tools/verify_rosters.py`.

## Running it

No dependencies — the engine is pure standard library, and works on Python 3.8+.

```bash
python3 run_tests.py                     # quick tier; --full before merging
```

```python
import dice
from combat_simulations import *

dice.seed(42)                            # optional: reproducible duels
prince = Character(name="Aenarion", faction_type="High Elves", profile_name="Prince",
                   Armor="Plate Armor", Weapon="Great Weapon")
warboss = Character(name="Grimgor", faction_type="Orcs", profile_name="Black Orc Warboss",
                    Weapon="Great Weapon")
combat_simulation(prince, warboss, rounds=4, verbose=True)
```

To measure a matchup rather than watch one fight, loop with `verbose=False` and rebuild both characters each iteration.
